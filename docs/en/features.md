# Features

> Early access: this describes version 0.1 for Windows. Details may change from one version to the next.

[← README](../../README.md) · [Français](../fr/features.md)

## New in version 0.2: one project at a time

- **Projects**: each project is a plain folder of `.md` files; only one is open at a time and the app shows nothing else. Closing a project takes a deliberate move (hold `Esc` for 2 seconds).
- **Parking** (`Ctrl+Space`): park a stray thought in `parking.md` without leaving your text, sort it later (tomorrow / card / trash).
- **Concentration** (`Ctrl+Shift+T`): full screen, only the paragraph you are writing stays sharp.
- **Session** (`Ctrl+Shift+S`): 10, 25 or 45 minutes; in draft mode delete, arrows and mouse are locked. A short **landing** screen follows.

## Today and planning

- **Three tasks a day, no more.** A short list gets finished.
- The **home screen** suggests a single next action, with a button to start focus.
- The **"Plan my day"** assistant: give your energy (1 to 5), write your priorities, mark each one *hard* or *light*; the app suggests an order. High energy: hardest first. Low energy: light first.
- A "first thing for tomorrow" written in the evening review is offered the next morning.

## Energy level

One tap from 1 to 5. Once enough readings are saved, the app works out your best period of the day, **only on your computer**. It is a feeling you report yourself: no sensor, no health data, no diagnosis.

## Focus mode

One task, one timer (15, 25, 50 or 90 minutes, adjustable). A small counter stays visible in the menu while you work elsewhere in the app. Completed minutes feed your evening review.

## Writing

- Full-screen editor with autosave; the note list is a drawer.
- Templates: daily journal, stuck/solved, weekly review, lecture notes (Cornell), reading notes, meeting.
- Open any `.md` / `.markdown` / `.txt` file (5 MB max): file dialog, folder browser, drag and drop, recent files.
- Reading mode (`Ctrl+E`): headings, checkboxes, quotes, code.
- A nano-style command bar lists the shortcuts ([shortcuts](shortcuts.md)).

## Revision cards

- Select text in a note, `Ctrl+J`: it becomes a card; the question is pre-filled with the heading above.
- Spaced repetition with three grades: again (tomorrow), good, easy (simplified SM-2).
- Import your own `.md` files as cards, by files or by whole folder (checklist). Duplicates are skipped; your originals are never modified.
- A library lists all cards, with a filter and access to the folder. File format: [data and formats](data-and-formats.md).

## Brain dump and evening review

- **Brain dump**: write everything without sorting; each line then becomes a task, a note, an idea or goes to the bin.
- **Evening review** (60 seconds): numbers of the day, one win, one blocker, the first thing for tomorrow. Saved as `notes/bilan-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Navigation and settings

- Eight-icon home screen; a directional pad (and arrow keys) moves the selection, OK opens, ◄ ► change section, ⌂ returns home.
- The menu folds into an icon strip (`Ctrl+B`).
- Settings (gear): paper or ink theme, text size, page width, default focus duration.

## What the app does not do

No sync, no account, no mobile version, no access to your email or calendars, no notifications, no streaks. This version has no AI feature and sends no data to any AI service. Windows only for now.
